Privacy: game-start notifications

Asking to be told when a game begins is always a free choice, and stopping is always one tap.

What is saved

A browser address: a long code that the browser makes for this site, with two keys that go with it. It is a browser address, not a name, an e-mail address or a phone number. Nothing else about the visitor is saved with it.

Who carries the message

Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft carry the short message to the browser, depending on the browser in use.

When it is saved, and for how long

Only after you tap the button and allow the browser's own question. Nothing is saved before that, and nothing is saved before the cookie question is answered. It is kept until you tap Stop notifications, or choose Only what is needed in Cookie choices, or the browser's provider tells the service that the address no longer works. Whichever comes first.

How to stop

Open the console's Home screen and tap Stop notifications. The address is removed from the service first, then from the browser. Choosing Only what is needed in Cookie choices does the same, and also erases the small yes or no note kept on this device.
